How this screen behaves
  • One click on a card opens Write in that mode. There is no Next button and nothing to keep selected.
  • Templates sit in their own row under the modes. Picking one opens Write with its questions and mode set. "See all" opens the full sheet.
  • Opening a draft skips this screen. In Write the mode shows small, with Change (screen 2).

  • The mode shows small at the top. Change opens a short menu with a tick on the current one (shown open here). Switching keeps your questions.
